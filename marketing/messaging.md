
# Targeted Marketing Messages Strategy

## Academics

**Problem:** You need to deeply analyze dense literature, but reading on a glowing monitor causes eye strain and limits retention.

**Solution:** Our service compiles your subscriptions into clean PDFs so you can print, physically highlight, and synthesize information without digital distractions.

**Key Message:** Turn endless browser tabs into focused, annotatable PDFs for deeper research.

## Digital Minimalists

**Problem:** You want to stay informed by your favorite writers but are exhausted by constant screen time and inbox clutter.

**Solution:** We batch your digital subscriptions into a single printable document, letting you catch up on reading entirely offline.

**Key Message:** Read your favorite newsletters offline and reclaim your screen-free time.

## Executives

**Problem:** You subscribe to essential industry newsletters but lack the time to read them amidst a chaotic, overflowing inbox.

**Solution:** We consolidate your must-read insights into one portable PDF that is perfect for uninterrupted reading on your next flight or commute.

**Key Message:** Consolidate your industry reading into one distraction-free brief for the commute.
